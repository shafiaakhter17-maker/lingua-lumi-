STORY 01

The Little Umbrella

Mina found a small yellow umbrella near the school gate. It was not raining, but she carried it home anyway. The next morning, rain filled the streets. Mina smiled and opened the little umbrella. She realized that sometimes a small thing can be useful at exactly the right moment.

STORY 02

The Old Letter

Leo found an old letter inside a wooden box. The paper was soft and yellow with age. He could not understand every word, but the message felt warm and kind. Leo carefully placed the letter back in the box. Some memories deserve to be protected.

STORY 03

The Window Garden

Aya placed three small pots beside her window. She planted mint, basil, and tiny flowers. Every morning she gave them water before breakfast. After several weeks, the window became bright and green. Aya discovered that caring for something every day can slowly create something beautiful.

STORY 04

The Quiet Library

Every Saturday, Aya visited the old library near her home. She loved the silence between the shelves. One day she chose a book about distant places. While reading, she imagined mountains, forests, and small villages. When she closed the book, she felt as if she had travelled without leaving her chair.

STORY 05

The Paper Moon

Sam made a moon from silver paper. He hung it above his desk with a thin piece of string. During the day, it looked simple. At night, the lamp made it shine softly. Sam loved looking at it while studying. It reminded him that imagination could turn something ordinary into something beautiful.

STORY 06

The Blue Painting

Eli painted a picture using only shades of blue. At first, he thought it looked too simple. His teacher asked him to look again. Eli noticed tiny details in the clouds, water, and sky. He smiled. Sometimes simplicity can hold more beauty than we expect.
